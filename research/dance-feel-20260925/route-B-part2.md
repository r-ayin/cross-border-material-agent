== A档续 ==
A8 捞臂过顶+摆胯 Scoop Arm into Hip Sway（3-4s）：scoop arm across chest→overhead→point front，然后摆胯groove；竖屏拉长线条，二段式小高潮。短语: she scoops one arm across her chest and sweeps it gracefully overhead, then points forward / arm framing movement flowing into a relaxed hip sway。来源 steezy.co/posts/10-easy-dance-moves-anyone-can-learn
A9 Whacking手臂线条+过顶框位 Lines & Overheads（2-3s）：单臂下指→侧指→上指卡拍干脆切换；双手颈后框头挺胸抬下巴；几何臂位截图传播价值强。短语: sharp straight arm lines hitting the beat, arm extending down, to the side, then up / she frames her head with both arms, chest open, chin lifted confidently。来源 steezy.co/posts/3-easy-waacking-whacking-moves-that-beginner-whackers-should-start-with
A10 傲娇走向镜头 Sassy Walk（2-3s，2-3步）：视线锁镜头，hip-led每步顶胯，直线走近=打破第四面墙钩子；走路是i2v最稳定动作之一。短语: she walks confidently toward the camera in a straight line, hips leading each step / sassy runway walk, the skirt sways with each step。来源 steezy.co/posts/how-to-dance-sexy
A11 交叉滑步 Mic Drop Cross Slide（BTS，3-4s）：左脚平贴地向右后交叉滑动45度+右脚跟内转，手臂侧抬随拍落下，肩前展微后仰；漂移质感，裙摆横扫方向性流线。短语: she slides one foot smoothly behind the other, gliding sideways with a flat foot / arm lifts then drops on the beat, shoulder flared, leaning back slightly with attitude。来源 steezy.co/posts/how-to-do-the-dance-move-from-mic-drop-by-bts
A12 慢速单圈转身 Slow Single Turn（裙摆大招，3-4s，360°禁多圈）：spotting视线锁镜头（未验证），一臂overhead为轴，前脚掌pivot；裙摆离心开花=money shot；减速面向镜头裙摆落下定格。短语: she performs one slow graceful turn on the balls of her feet, one arm raised overhead / the pleated skirt flares outward in a smooth arc as she turns, then settles softly as she faces the camera。来源 blog.csdn.net/weixin_29641609/article/details/164314424（裙摆旋转弧度佐证）
A13 撩发+结尾Pose Hair Tuck & Final Pose（1.5-2.5s）：一手缓慢把头发别耳后，歪头对镜微笑/wink，重心单腿顶胯定格；最后2秒决定完播/重播/截图。短语: she slowly tucks a strand of hair behind her ear, smiling at the camera / final pose with weight on one leg, hip slightly out, playful wink, skirt settling softly。来源 steezy.co/posts/how-to-dance-sexy

== B档 高崩坏风险（15s单镜头i2v禁用或降级）==
B1 Humpty跳跃交叉腿：跳跃易腿部扭曲多肢，降级=胯圈+屈膝去跳跃。
B2 快速旋转/多圈pirouette：裙摆实心锥/穿腿/背景拖影，降级=A12慢速单圈。
B3 Kangsta Wok深蹲/快速level drop：腰腿折叠畸变，降级=浅屈膝dip约20cm上身直立。
B4 高频手臂wave（2秒3次）：手指手腕必崩，降级=每2-3秒一次慢速wave。
B5 Booty Pop手按膝：手-膝精确接触易穿模，降级=去手按膝只留侧向伸腿。
B6 Smeeze快速手脚异步交替：缠绕肢体重灾区，禁用。
B7 一切地板动作（坐/卧/落地）：i2v地面接触物理极差+长裙铺开穿模，完全禁用。
B8 剧烈甩发+旋转复合：甩发遮脸五官崩，降级=静止时轻甩一次。

== 组合套路设计原则 ==
①每2-4秒一个主导动作、全片4-6段，动作切换点即卡点；②动态曲线小-中-大-小，高潮（旋转/大幅摆胯）放8-12s黄金分割位；③结尾必回镜头前定格（完播钩子）；④全取A档，崩了同档替换。

方案1「女神裙摆流」（优雅向，风险最低，推荐首选）：0-2s A3 two-step轻bounce → 2-5s A1 figure-8摆胯视线锁镜头 → 5-8s A5手臂波浪→A9过顶框位 → 8-12s A12慢速单圈转身裙摆开花高潮 → 12-15s A13撩发+顶胯定格。
prompt骨架: A young woman in a pink high-waisted pleated maxi skirt dances in one continuous shot facing the camera: first a gentle side-to-side two-step groove, then a slow figure-8 hip sway, then a fluid arm wave flowing into an overhead arm frame, then one slow graceful turn making the pleated skirt flare in a smooth arc, finally she tucks her hair behind her ear and holds a confident final pose with weight on one leg. Fixed camera, smooth controlled motion, natural relaxed hands, the fabric flows and settles naturally around her legs, her body proportions stay consistent throughout.

方案2「卡点网感」（KPOP副歌感）：0-2s A3 bounce → 2-4.5s A2肩部shimmy+hit×2（双手叉腰展示高腰）→ 4.5-7.5s A8捞臂过顶→摆胯 → 7.5-10.5s A7 hip roll×2圈裙摆锥形 → 10.5-13s A10 sassy walk逼近镜头 → 13-15s 停步歪头微笑定格。

方案3「滑步酷girl」（街头Mic Drop记忆点）：0-2.5s A3+A2 → 2.5-6.5s A11交叉滑步左右各一次裙摆横扫 → 6.5-9.5s A1摆胯+A9单臂过顶框位 → 9.5-13s A12慢速单圈转身 → 13-15s 背对镜头回眸定格。

方案4「零风险保底」（反复崩坏时用）：0-3s A3原地two-step+摆臂 → 3-6s A2肩部shimmy下半身近静止 → 6-9s A5+A9纯上身波浪与框位 → 9-12s A1小幅摆胯无脚步 → 12-15s A13撩发定格。全程无转身无走位双脚原地，仍保留4个卡点切换。
